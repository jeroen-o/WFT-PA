Complaints

Complaints procedure

Not satisfied with Wft Permanent Actueel? Let us know and we will look for a solution together.

How to file a complaint

  1. Email your complaint to info@dfobv.nl or call +31 33 258 04 60.
  2. State your name, your email address and what your complaint is about. If it is about a quiz question, state the module and the question.
  3. We will confirm that we have received your complaint, investigate it and let you know what we do with it.

Error in a question

An error in a question is not a complaint but a report, and it is welcome. In the quiz, every question has the link “Doubt about this question? Email it straight to DFO”.

Can’t work it out together?

See the complaints procedure of DFO BV at www.dfobv.nl.
