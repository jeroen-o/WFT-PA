Wft, PE and Permanent Actueel

As a financial adviser in the Netherlands you must remain professionally competent: you hold a Wft diploma and keep your knowledge up to date with permanent education (PE). PE points no longer exist under the Wft; since 1 January 2014 you take one PE exam per professional qualification within each PE period, at an exam institute recognised by the CDFD. The current PE period runs until 31 March 2028 inclusive. The PA test is a practice test and earns no PE points or PE exam.

According to the AFM, staying permanently up to date applies to all employees with substantive client contact, including back office and support staff. Record which knowledge you have gained: keep your certificates in your competence or personnel file.
