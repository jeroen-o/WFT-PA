Wft Mortgage Credit

Wft Mortgage Credit at a glance

  • Who needs it: required for 1 professional qualification: Mortgage Credit Adviser.
  • PE: you take one PE exam per professional qualification within the PE period, which runs until 31 March 2028 inclusive.
  • Exam terms: the final objectives and exam terms of the CDFD have been renewed for exams from 1 April 2026.
  • Current: TRHK and NHG Terms &amp; Standards: they change every year on 1 January.
  • Practise: 7 multiple-choice questions with an explanation for every answer, free and without an account. Last updated 18 October 2026.

What is covered

  • Lending standards: debt-service ratio and loan-to-value
  • Mortgage types and interest types
  • Owner-occupied home rules: repayment requirement, deduction of borrowing, owner-occupied home reserve
  • National Mortgage Guarantee (NHG)
  • Valuation, notary and mortgage deed
  • Compensation for early repayment
  • ESIS and providing information
  • Income and creditworthiness
  • Risks in the advice: death, incapacity for work, unemployment, end of relationship, retirement
  • Sustainability and energy label

What’s new in 2026

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • TRHK and NHG Terms & Standards. They change every year on 1 January. Always work with the annual version.
  • Mortgage acceptance 2026. The NHG cost limit is €470,000. Several lenders limit the interest-only part of new applications to 30% of the property value, and valuation reports must address foundation risks. Check the current criteria per lender.

The final objectives and exam terms of the CDFD for this module are divided into tasks:

  1. Gathering the client’s data
  2. Drawing up the risk analysis for the advice
  3. Advising (and where applicable intermediating) a suitable solution, both financial and organisational
  4. Managing and keeping the advice up to date

Example questions Wft Mortgage Credit

This is what the quiz questions look like. In the quiz the answers appear in random order.

Example question 1 exam term 1h.4

Peter (58) and Annemiek (57) live in a detached house in Hilversum. Now that the children have left home, they are buying a holiday home in Zeeland, where they stay about ten weekends a year. They finance that home with a separate mortgage at their own bank. They hope to be able to deduct that interest too. What is correct?

  • Both homes count as owner-occupied homes, because they both stay in them regularly and for longer periods
  • For the holiday home they pay notional rental value instead of box 3 tax
  • The holiday home and the loan fall in box 3; the interest is not deductible in box 1
  • The interest on the holiday home is deductible, but at a lower rate
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: The holiday home and the loan fall in box 3; the interest is not deductible in box 1. Only the main residence is an owner-occupied home; a second home falls in box 3 together with the debt.

  • Both homes count as owner-occupied homes, because they both stay in them regularly and for longer periods: There can be only one owner-occupied home: the main residence.
  • The interest on the holiday home is deductible, but at a lower rate: For a second home there is no interest deduction in box 1, not even at a lower rate.
  • For the holiday home they pay notional rental value instead of box 3 tax: The notional rental value applies only to the owner-occupied home (the main residence).

Example question 2 exam term 1i.4

Bas (35) is an account manager with €65,000 gross per year and Merel (34) is a part-time teacher with €30,000 gross per year. They want to buy a family home in Amersfoort. Merel has heard that the second income counts only partly for the mortgage. They ask their adviser how that works. How does Merel's income count towards the test income under the lending standards?

  • For half, to absorb the risk of the second income dropping out, on the lender's side
  • In full, like the highest income; the restriction on the second income has lapsed
  • Not at all; only the highest income determines their maximum mortgage
  • Only if both partners have a permanent contract of indefinite duration
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: In full, like the highest income; the restriction on the second income has lapsed. Since 2024 the income of the lower-earning partner counts in full towards the test income.

  • For half, to absorb the risk of the second income dropping out, on the lender's side: Partial weighting applied in the past; since 2024 the second income counts in full.
  • Not at all; only the highest income determines their maximum mortgage: The second income also counts towards the test income.
  • Only if both partners have a permanent contract of indefinite duration: A permanent contract is not a condition for counting the second income.

Example question 3 exam term 1d.2

Stijn (35) is a teacher and is buying an apartment in Zwolle on his own with a fully annuity mortgage. He has used all his savings for the purchase costs and furnishing. His car is old and his central heating boiler will soon need replacing. He assumes he can pay for unexpected costs from his salary. What is an important point of attention in the advice?

  • That he had better make the mortgage interest-only in order to build up assets faster
  • That he is obliged to take out an investment insurance alongside the mortgage
  • That his interest deduction lapses after ten years and his costs then rise
  • The importance of building up a financial buffer for unexpected expenses
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: The importance of building up a financial buffer for unexpected expenses. Without a buffer a setback can lead directly to payment problems; the adviser should discuss this.

  • That he had better make the mortgage interest-only in order to build up assets faster: An interest-only loan is not owner-occupied home debt and increases the risk at the end.
  • That his interest deduction lapses after ten years and his costs then rise: The interest deduction runs for a maximum of thirty years, not ten.
  • That he is obliged to take out an investment insurance alongside the mortgage: An investment insurance is never mandatory alongside an annuity mortgage.
