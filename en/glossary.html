Wft glossary

The most important terms from the Wft, permanent education and the Wft modules, briefly explained. A handy memory aid while practising. Dutch terms are given in brackets.

Legislation as at 15 October 2026

A B C D F G I K L M N O P R S T U W
Acceptance obligation (acceptatieplicht)
The obligation of a health insurer to accept everyone for the basic insurance, without a premium difference based on health. Wft Health Insurance
AFM
Netherlands Authority for the Financial Markets: the conduct supervisor. The AFM supervises how financial firms behave towards clients, grants licences to advisers and intermediaries and supervises professional competence. Wft Basic
AMLR
The EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation. From 10 July 2027 the core rules on customer due diligence sit in this directly applicable regulation. Wft Basic · Wft Wealth
AO/IC
Administrative organisation and internal control (administratieve organisatie en interne controle): the description of a firm’s business processes, including how professional competence and staying permanently up to date are arranged. Wft Basic
AOV
Disability insurance (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering): a private insurance with which a self-employed person insures their income in the event of incapacity for work. Wft Income
AOW
General Old Age Pensions Act (Algemene Ouderdomswet): the basic state pension from the state pension age. Wft Pensions
AVB
Business liability insurance (aansprakelijkheidsverzekering voor bedrijven): covers damage to persons or property of others for which a business is liable. Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial)
Benefits-in-kind policy (naturapolis)
A health insurance under which the insurer makes agreements with care providers. With a non-contracted care provider the reimbursement can be lower. Wft Health Insurance
BGfo
Financial Supervision (Conduct of Business) Decree: the general administrative order with the conduct rules on, among other things, duty of care, provision of information, remuneration and business operations. Wft Basic · Wft Mortgage Credit
BKR
Dutch credit registration bureau (Bureau Krediet Registratie): registers consumer credit. Lenders consult the register when assessing an application. Wft Consumer Credit · Wft Mortgage Credit
Bogus self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid)
When someone formally works as a freelancer but the working relationship is in reality an employment relationship. The Tax Administration has enforced this again since 1 January 2025. Wft Income
Box 3
The box in income tax for income from savings and investments. A return on the assets on the reference date is taxed. Wft Wealth
Business interruption insurance (bedrijfsschadeverzekering)
Compensates the loss of gross profit and continuing fixed costs if the business comes to a standstill after an insured event, such as fire, during the indemnity period. Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial)
CCD II
The revised European Consumer Credit Directive (2023/2225). It applies from 20 November 2026 and brings, among other things, buy now pay later via a third party and lease with a purchase option under the credit regime. Wft Consumer Credit
CDFD
Dutch Financial Services Competence Board (College Deskundigheid Financiële Dienstverlening). The CDFD sets the final objectives and exam terms of the Wft exams and recognises the exam institutes that conduct Wft and PE exams. Wft Basic
Client profile (klantprofiel)
The picture of the client that the adviser records: financial position, knowledge and experience, objectives and risk appetite. It is the basis for suitable advice. Wft Basic
Commission ban (provisieverbod)
The ban on receiving commission from the provider for certain products, such as mortgages and complex products. The client then pays the adviser directly. Wft Basic · Wft Mortgage Credit
DAC8
European rules under which, since 1 January 2026, crypto service providers pass data about their clients to the tax authorities. Wft Wealth
DNB
De Nederlandsche Bank: the prudential supervisor. DNB monitors the financial soundness of banks, insurers and pension funds. Wft Basic · Wft Pensions
DORA
Digital Operational Resilience Act: European rules on the digital resilience of financial institutions, applicable since 17 January 2025. Small and medium-sized insurance intermediaries fall outside it. Wft Basic
Duty of care (zorgplicht)
The obligation of a financial service provider to act with due care and to take the client’s interests into account, among other things by giving suitable advice. Wft Basic
Duty of disclosure (mededelingsplicht)
The legal duty of the policyholder to report, before the insurance is concluded, all facts they know that are relevant to the insurer’s decision. Wft Non-life Insurance (Personal) · Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial) · Wft Income
Final objective and exam term (eindterm en toetsterm)
Final objectives describe what a candidate must be able to do; exam terms elaborate these into testable knowledge and skills. The CDFD sets them; the current ones apply to exams from 1 April 2026. Wft Basic
GLOM
EBA Guidelines on loan origination and monitoring (EBA/GL/2020/06). They are aimed at credit institutions and lenders. Wft Mortgage Credit · Wft Consumer Credit
IDD
Insurance Distribution Directive: the European directive on the distribution of insurance, with requirements on, among other things, information, conflicts of interest and professional competence. Wft Basic · Wft Non-life Insurance (Personal) · Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial)
IVA
Income provision for fully incapacitated persons (Inkomensvoorziening volledig arbeidsongeschikten): the WIA benefit for those who are fully and permanently incapacitated for work. Wft Income
Kifid
Dutch Financial Services Complaints Institute: handles complaints from consumers about financial service providers if they cannot resolve them with the provider itself. Wft Basic
Lending standards (leennormen)
The rules that determine how much someone may borrow at most for an owner-occupied home: the debt-service ratio relative to income and the loan relative to the property value. They are set every year. Wft Mortgage Credit
Mandatory deductible (eigen risico, Zvw)
The amount per calendar year that an insured person aged 18 or over pays themselves for care from the basic package, with exceptions such as the GP. The amount is set every year. Wft Health Insurance
MiCA
Markets in Crypto-Assets: the European regulation for crypto services. Since 30 December 2024, providers of crypto services in the EU need a licence. Wft Wealth
NHG
National Mortgage Guarantee (Nationale Hypotheek Garantie): a guarantee for mortgages up to a limit set each year. In a forced sale with a residual debt, the guarantee can take over that debt under conditions. Wft Mortgage Credit
OVI
Passenger accident insurance (ongevallenverzekering inzittenden): pays a fixed amount on death or permanent disability of passengers after a traffic accident, regardless of who is at fault. Wft Non-life Insurance (Personal)
PE exam
Exam for permanent education. Anyone who advises takes one PE exam per professional qualification within each PE period, at an exam institute recognised by the CDFD. It mainly tests current developments. Wft Basic
PE period
The period within which you take your PE exams. The current PE period runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028 inclusive. Wft Basic
PE points
A points system for permanent education that no longer exists under the Wft. Since 1 January 2014 you keep your advising authority valid with PE exams. Wft Basic
Permanently up to date (permanent actueel)
The requirement that employees with substantive client contact keep their professional knowledge up to date and can apply it. It is an open standard: the firm decides how to implement it and is responsible for it. Wft Basic
Product liability (productaansprakelijkheid)
The liability of a producer for damage caused by a defective product, without fault having to be proved. Under the new EU directive, software also counts as a product. Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial)
Professional qualification (beroepskwalificatie)
The combination of Wft modules you need to advise in a certain field, for example Mortgage Credit Adviser (Basic, Wealth and Mortgage Credit). Wft Basic
Reimbursement policy (restitutiepolis)
A health insurance under which the insured chooses the care provider themselves and the costs are reimbursed up to a maximum market rate. Wft Health Insurance
Repayment requirement (aflossingseis)
To qualify for mortgage interest relief on a mortgage taken out after 2012, the loan must be repaid at least on an annuity basis within a maximum of 30 years. Wft Mortgage Credit
Risk profile (risicoprofiel)
The extent to which a client wants and is able to take risk when investing. The adviser determines it on the basis of objectives, financial position, knowledge and experience. Wft Wealth
SVI
Passenger damage insurance (schadeverzekering inzittenden): compensates the actual damage of passengers after a traffic accident, such as personal injury and damage to belongings, regardless of who is at fault. Wft Non-life Insurance (Personal)
Transfer of accrued rights (invaren)
Converting accrued pension rights to the new pension system of the Wtp. Wft Pensions
TRHK
Temporary Regulation on Mortgage Credit (Tijdelijke regeling hypothecair krediet): the ministerial regulation with the statutory lending standards for mortgages. It is revised every year on 1 January. Wft Mortgage Credit
Underinsurance (onderverzekering)
If the insured amount is lower than the actual value, the insurer compensates the damage proportionally, unless the policy gives a guarantee against underinsurance. Wft Non-life Insurance (Personal) · Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial)
UPO
Uniform Pension Overview (Uniform Pensioenoverzicht): the annual overview from the pension provider with the accrued and attainable pension rights. Wft Pensions
Wft
Financial Supervision Act (Wet op het financieel toezicht): the main act for supervision of the financial sector, with rules on, among other things, licences, professional competence, provision of information and duty of care. Wft Basic
WGA
Resumption of work by partially fit persons (Werkhervatting gedeeltelijk arbeidsgeschikten): the WIA scheme for those who are partially or not permanently incapacitated for work. Wft Income
WIA
Work and Income according to Labour Capacity Act: the scheme for employees who are incapacitated for work after two years of sickness, with the IVA and the WGA. Wft Income
Wtp
Pension Transition Act (Wet toekomst pensioenen): the new pension system with defined contribution schemes. Pension schemes must have moved by 1 January 2028 at the latest. Wft Pensions
Wwft
Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act. Among other things, it requires customer due diligence and the reporting of unusual transactions. Wft Basic · Wft Wealth
