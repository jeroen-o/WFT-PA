What’s new: Q4 2026

What’s new for financial advisers in Q4 2026? Per Wft module the changes in legislation, exam terms and the market that you need to know to stay permanently up to date.

Important dates

Coming up

  • 20 November 2026CCD II applies. The revised Consumer Credit Directive: stricter creditworthiness assessment, including BNPL and lease with a purchase option. Wft Consumer Credit
  • 9 December 2026Transposition of the new Product Liability Directive. Deadline for the Dutch law; software counts as a product. Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial), Wft Non-life Insurance (Personal)
  • 1 January 2027Annual changes. New annual versions of, among others, the lending standards, NHG standards, health premiums and deductible, and tax amounts. Wft Mortgage Credit, Wft Health Insurance, Wft Wealth, Wft Income, Wft Pensions
  • 1 April 2027PE exams updated again. The CDFD updates the PE exams every year on 1 April and invited developments to be submitted for the exams from 1 April 2027.
  • 10 July 2027EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR) applies. The core customer due diligence rules sit in a directly applicable EU regulation. Wft Basic, Wft Wealth, Wft Mortgage Credit, Wft Consumer Credit
  • 1 January 2028Deadline for the transition to the new pension system. Pension schemes must have moved to the system of the Pension Transition Act. Wft Pensions
  • 31 March 2028End of the 2025–2028 PE period. Deadline for taking your PE exam per professional qualification.

Recently took effect

  • 1 April 2026New CDFD exam terms. Renewed final objectives and exam terms for all Wft modules.
  • 1 January 2026DAC8 applies. Crypto service providers in the EU pass client data to the tax authorities. Wft Wealth
  • 1 April 2025Start of the 2025–2028 PE period. Within this period you take one PE exam per professional qualification.
  • 17 January 2025DORA applies. European rules on the digital operational resilience of financial institutions. Wft Basic
  • 1 January 2025Enforcement against bogus self-employment. The Tax Administration enforces the assessment of employment relationships again. Wft Income
  • 30 December 2024MiCA fully applicable. Providers of crypto services in the EU need a licence. Wft Wealth

Wft Basic

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • EU anti-money laundering package (AMLR and AMLD6). It comes on top of the current Wwft, which will in time be replaced by an implementation act. The gatekeeper role of advisers changes accordingly.
  • EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR) from 10 July 2027. The core customer due diligence rules will then sit in a directly applicable EU regulation. The new European anti-money laundering authority AMLA has been established and will directly supervise a limited number of large institutions; for advisory firms the national supervisor remains the point of contact.
  • DORA has applied since 17 January 2025. The European rules on digital operational resilience apply to, among others, banks and insurers. Insurance intermediaries that are micro, small or medium-sized enterprises fall outside it, but they notice it through their suppliers and principals. The Wft requirement of controlled business operations still applies to ICT risks.
  • AI Act. Phased application affects advisory firms through software, models and outsourcing.

Wft Consumer Credit

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • CCD II from 20 November 2026. The revised Consumer Credit Directive will apply; the Dutch implementation act is still in preparation. Among other things, BNPL and rent or lease with a purchase option come under the credit regime.
  • Stricter creditworthiness assessment under CCD II. Credit may only be granted if the creditworthiness assessment shows that the consumer is likely to be able to meet their obligations. There will also be stricter requirements for advertising and pre-contractual information.

Wft Non-life Insurance (Personal)

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • Changed exam term. Exam term 1d.9 was changed on 1 April 2026: more room for questions about passenger accident insurance (OVI) and passenger damage insurance (SVI), the differences between the two, and which form fits the client’s needs.
  • No major change in the law identified. The framework remains Book 7 of the Civil Code (insurance contract), the IDD and the Wft conduct rules. Policy conditions differ per insurer; always check the current conditions.

Wft Non-life Insurance (Commercial)

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • AI Act and DORA. They affect business clients and their insurance needs, including for cyber and IT risks.
  • New Product Liability Directive. EU Directive 2024/2853 must be transposed into Dutch law by 9 December 2026 and applies to products placed on the market after that date. Software, including AI systems, then counts as a product. Discuss with clients who make software or use it in their products whether their liability cover matches.
  • Sanctions legislation. Remains a standing point of attention for business clients and foreign interests.

Wft Wealth

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • Box 3. The Box 3 Transitional Act applies until a new system takes effect; legal redress following the “Christmas ruling” (Kerstarrest) is also under way. Work with the rules of the relevant tax year.
  • Crypto: MiCA and DAC8. Since 30 December 2024, providers of crypto services in the EU need a licence under the MiCA Regulation; in the Netherlands the AFM grants it. A licence does not protect against price losses and there is no investor compensation scheme for crypto. Since 1 January 2026, crypto service providers in the EU pass information about their clients to the tax authorities (DAC8).
  • EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR) from 10 July 2027. The customer due diligence rules will sit in a directly applicable EU regulation.

Wft Mortgage Credit

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • TRHK and NHG Terms & Standards. They change every year on 1 January. Always work with the annual version.
  • Mortgage acceptance 2026. The NHG cost limit is €470,000. Several lenders limit the interest-only part of new applications to 30% of the property value, and valuation reports must address foundation risks. Check the current criteria per lender.

Wft Income

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • Changed exam term. Exam term 1h.8 moved from knowledge level to understanding level on 1 April 2026: you must not only be able to describe the legal frameworks for asking for medical information, medical examination limits and medical acceptance, but also explain and apply them in a concrete situation, such as hereditary conditions and a health declaration or medical examination.
  • Disability insurance for the self-employed. A compulsory basic insurance is in preparation. Follow the status of the bill; the current rules remain in force until introduction.
  • Enforcement against bogus self-employment since 1 January 2025. The Tax Administration enforces the assessment of employment relationships again. If a freelancer is in reality an employee, the employee insurances apply. Discuss with freelance clients how certain their status is; it affects their income risk and the choice of disability insurance.

Wft Pensions

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • Pension Transition Act. Pension schemes must have moved to the new system by 1 January 2028 at the latest. Watch transition moments, survivor cover and communication to participants.

Wft Health Insurance

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • Annual changes on 1 January. Premiums, the mandatory deductible, the basic package and the healthcare allowance are set anew each year. Use the amounts of the relevant year.
