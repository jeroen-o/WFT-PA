Frequently asked questions

What is permanent education (PE)?

Anyone who advises in financial services must be and remain professionally competent. For that you need a Wft diploma and you keep your knowledge up to date with permanent education. Within each PE period you take one PE exam per professional qualification; the current period runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028 inclusive. You can find the current period and requirements at the CDFD.

Does this test count towards my PE?

No. The PA test is a practice test. It helps you test and maintain your knowledge, but it is not a recognised PE exam and does not count towards the registration of your professional competence. You take your PE exam at an exam centre recognised by the CDFD.

Does the test earn PE points?

No. Under the Wft there are no PE points any more: you keep your diplomas valid with PE exams. The PA test is a free practice test and earns no PE points or PE exam. Read more in PE points and Wft Permanent Actueel.

What does it cost?

Nothing. All modules and the test are free for all advisers, without a subscription and without an account. Wft Permanent Actueel is an activity of DFO BV.

How does the test work?

You choose one or more modules. With one module you get 7 random questions, and for each module you add, 5 extra: 2 modules give 12 questions, 3 modules 17, all 9 modules 47. After each answer you see the explanation. Afterwards you receive a certificate with your result, as a pdf in your mail or to print. You can take the test as often as you like; you get different questions each time.

How often should I take the test?

Take the test every quarter, so you stay permanently up to date. On the homepage and in the test you set a reminder in your calendar with one click: every quarter, for the next 5 years, with a link to the test. Keep every certificate in your personnel file and also hand it to your employer. That shows you keep your knowledge up to date. The certificate does not replace your PE exam.

What is on the certificate?

Your name, the module, your result, the date, the date on which the questions of that module were last updated and a certificate number. A copy of the certificate goes to DFO BV (info@dfobv.nl) as soon as you send the email message.

Why do I have to enter my details to see my result?

This lets us prepare your certificate as a pdf in an email to yourself, with a copy to DFO BV (info@dfobv.nl). You enter your name and email address and agree to that copy; date of birth, company and your employer’s email address are optional. Practice rounds, such as practising your wrong questions again or practising per exam term, do not ask for any details.

What happens to my data?

PA-WFT.nl itself stores nothing and sends nothing to a server automatically: your browser creates the certificate. The certificate is prepared in an email to yourself with a copy to DFO BV (info@dfobv.nl); you send that message yourself from your email programme. See the privacy statement.

Which modules do I need?

That depends on your professional qualifications. A professional qualification consists of a combination of modules, for example Mortgage Credit Adviser: Basic, Wealth and Mortgage Credit. Choose your qualifications in the package check on the homepage and check the requirements that apply to you at the CDFD.

How up to date are the questions?

For each module you can see when the questions were last updated. The reference date of the legislation is 15 October 2026. Amounts and percentages that change every year are deliberately not in the questions.

I see an error in a question. What now?

We would like to hear about it. Email the question and what you think is wrong to info@dfobv.nl.

Please note. The PA test is a practice test and does not count as a Wft exam, PE exam or PE evidence.
