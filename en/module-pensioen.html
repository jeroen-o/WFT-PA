Wft Pensions at a glance

  • Who needs it: required for 1 professional qualification: Pensions Adviser.
  • PE: you take one PE exam per professional qualification within the PE period, which runs until 31 March 2028 inclusive.
  • Exam terms: the final objectives and exam terms of the CDFD have been renewed for exams from 1 April 2026.
  • Current: Pension Transition Act: pension schemes must have moved to the new system by 1 January 2028 at the latest.
  • Practise: 7 multiple-choice questions with an explanation for every answer, free and without an account. Last updated 18 October 2026.

What is covered

  • The three pillars and the AOW
  • Pensions Act: pension agreement, providers, information and transfer of value
  • Pension Transition Act: defined contribution schemes, transition and transfer of accrued rights (invaren)
  • Partner’s and orphan’s pension, Anw gap
  • Tax framework (Witteveen) and annuities
  • Pensions on divorce
  • Pensions of directors-major shareholders (dga) and pensions in own management
  • Duty of care and advice on choices

Example questions Wft Pensions

This is what the quiz questions look like. In the quiz the answers appear in random order.

Example question 1 exam term 1b.1

Adviser Mirjam is preparing a new pension scheme at a bakery incorporated as a BV (private limited company). The manager wants to sign the pension agreement on behalf of the BV and says he 'always arranges that sort of thing'. Mirjam consults the Trade Register of the Chamber of Commerce. It shows that only the two directors together are authorised to represent the BV. What should Mirjam do?

  • Have it signed by the directors who are authorised according to the Trade Register, or by someone with their power of attorney
  • Have the agreement signed on behalf of the BV by the pension provider
  • Have the signature confirmed by the works council, which can then represent the BV
  • Nothing; a manager is always authorised to sign pension agreements on behalf of the BV
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: Have it signed by the directors who are authorised according to the Trade Register, or by someone with their power of attorney. Only someone who is authorised to represent the BV, or who has a power of attorney for it, can validly bind the BV.

  • Nothing; a manager is always authorised to sign pension agreements on behalf of the BV: A manager is not automatically authorised to represent; that appears from the Trade Register or a power of attorney.
  • Have the signature confirmed by the works council, which can then represent the BV: The works council has co-determination rights but does not represent the BV.
  • Have the agreement signed on behalf of the BV by the pension provider: The provider is another party and cannot represent the BV.

Example question 2 exam term 1g.3

The metal company of Bart Kok (58) has an average-salary scheme with an insurer. Pension costs have risen sharply in recent years. Bart wants to unilaterally scale the scheme back to a contribution scheme. The pension agreement contains an amendment clause (wijzigingsbeding). When may he invoke that clause?

  • Always, because the employer pays the premium and therefore decides on the content
  • Never; a pension scheme cannot be amended once introduced, even with an amendment clause
  • Only if the employees do not object within a month after written notice
  • Only in the case of an interest so compelling that the employees' interest must reasonably yield
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: Only in the case of an interest so compelling that the employees' interest must reasonably yield. A unilateral amendment through the amendment clause is only possible in the case of such a compelling interest of the employer.

  • Always, because the employer pays the premium and therefore decides on the content: Paying the premium gives the employer no unlimited right to amend unilaterally.
  • Only if the employees do not object within a month after written notice: Silence on the part of employees is not a statutory standard for a unilateral amendment.
  • Never; a pension scheme cannot be amended once introduced, even with an amendment clause: With an amendment clause amendment is possible, but only in the case of a compelling interest.

Example question 3 exam term 1h.18

Willem (54) and Saar (52) divorced two years ago. They reported the divorce to Willem's pension fund in time, so Saar is entitled to equalisation. Willem's pension scheme includes a partner's pension. Willem dies unexpectedly, well before his retirement date. What is Saar in principle entitled to?

  • An Anw benefit as Willem's divorced partner
  • Half of Willem's full old-age pension, payable from his retirement date
  • Payment of the cash-in value of Willem's accrued pension
  • The special partner's pension (bijzonder partnerpensioen), not part of Willem's old-age pension
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: The special partner's pension (bijzonder partnerpensioen), not part of Willem's old-age pension. Because Willem dies before his retirement date, no old-age pension is paid out; Saar is, however, entitled to the special partner's pension.

  • Half of Willem's full old-age pension, payable from his retirement date: Equalisation follows Willem's old-age pension; if he dies before the retirement date, it is not paid out.
  • An Anw benefit as Willem's divorced partner: A divorced partner is not a surviving dependant within the meaning of the Anw.
  • Payment of the cash-in value of Willem's accrued pension: Cashing in the pension for the ex-partner is not a consequence of death.
