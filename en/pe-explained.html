PE explained

PE points, PE exams and Wft Permanent Actueel

Short answer: under the Wft there are no PE points any more. Since 1 January 2014 you keep your advising authority valid with one PE exam per professional qualification within each PE period; the current period runs until 31 March 2028 inclusive. Wft Permanent Actueel helps you practise for free; the quiz itself earns no PE points or PE exam.

Do PE points still exist under the Wft?

No. Since 1 January 2014, permanent education under the Wft no longer works with PE points. You keep your advising authority valid by taking the PE exam for each professional qualification you hold within each PE period. You may still encounter PE points with voluntary professional registers; which activities count there is decided by each register.

What is PE under the Wft?

PE stands for permanent education. Anyone who advises on financial products must demonstrably be professionally competent: the right Wft diploma for every product-service combination in which you advise, and keeping that diploma valid with PE exams. You take the exams at an exam institute recognised by the CDFD. The AFM supervises the firm’s compliance with the diploma requirement.

What does the CDFD do?

The Dutch Financial Services Competence Board (CDFD) supports the Minister of Finance with the professional competence requirements in the Wft. It has three core tasks: (1) each year mapping relevant developments in the field and advising the minister on the final objectives and exam terms, which the minister sets by ministerial regulation after consultation; (2) the substantive management of the central exam bank for the initial exams, the PE exams and the special exams, including drawing up and maintaining exam questions; and (3) the recognition of and supervision over exam institutes, which may only conduct exams after recognition. In addition, the CDFD recognises foreign professional qualifications from other EU member states and Switzerland. Via Mijn Wft (with DigiD) you can see which professional qualifications you hold and download your diplomas and certificates. According to the AFM, you can see on the DUO website which diplomas are registered and until which date they are valid.

How long is the PE period?

According to the CDFD, the current PE period runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028 inclusive; the next runs from 1 April 2028 to 31 March 2031 inclusive. You take one PE exam per professional qualification within the period. When you do it is up to you, as long as it is before the end date. See When do I have to take the PE exam? (in Dutch)

What does a PE exam test?

The PE exam tests recent developments in the field. The exam terms are updated every year on 1 April; on 1 April 2026 the PE exams were renewed. According to the CDFD, these are developments that fit the module, are relevant to the client’s interest and are definitively known in time. See the CDFD news item (in Dutch).

Which developments appear in the PE exam?

According to the CDFD, a development can appear in the PE exams if it fits the scope of the Wft module, is relevant from the client’s perspective and has an impact on advisory practice, is no older than about three years on 1 January 2026 (or is relevant again) and is definitively known on 1 January 2026; the effective date may be later. Grounds include national legislation, European regulations, ministry policy, official guidelines or codes of conduct (for example from the AFM or the Association of Insurers), company schemes of a general nature and case law or Kifid rulings. For a European directive whose national rules are not yet final, only the content of the directive is tested.

Does permanently up to date also apply to employees without an advisory diploma?

Yes. The AFM distinguishes two layers. All client-facing employees with substantive client contact must be permanently up to date: they know the current developments in their field and can apply them. That also applies to back office staff and support staff who talk to clients about products. Anyone who advises also needs a valid professional qualification and keeps it valid with PE exams (the diploma requirement). The obligation to be permanently up to date is an open standard: the firm decides how to implement it, and the licence holder is responsible for it. For employees without a diploma requirement, the firm arranges professional competence through its business operations, for example with training that fits their work. See the AFM professional competence requirements (in Dutch).

How do you demonstrate that you are permanently up to date?

The firm describes in its business processes (AO/IC) how professional competence is arranged. According to the AFM, it helps to record for each employee which knowledge they have gained and whether they apply it in practice. A competence file per employee contains, for example, the role, the professional qualifications and PE exams, training followed with date and subject, and an annual evaluation. You can keep the PA test certificate in it as evidence that you keep your knowledge up to date; it does not replace the PE exam. In an investigation the AFM can ask for the AO/IC description, the overview of diplomas and PE and the evidence of staying permanently up to date.

Is exemption from the diploma requirement possible?

The AFM has an exemption policy for the diploma requirement in specific situations. Read that policy at the AFM before you rely on it.

What does Wft Permanent Actueel (PA) mean?

Permanent Actueel means that your professional knowledge stays up to date after your Wft diploma. PE exams mainly test what has changed in legislation and in the market. Wft Permanent Actueel (PA-WFT.nl) is a free practice environment from DFO BV where you practise per Wft module.

Which PE exams do I need?

That depends on your professional qualifications. The system is modular: you take an initial exam per module, and a combination of modules gives a professional qualification. According to the AFM these are: Health Insurance Adviser (Health Insurance), Consumer Credit Adviser (Basic, Consumer Credit), Personal Non-life Insurance Adviser (Basic, Non-life Insurance Personal), Wealth Adviser (Basic, Wealth), Income Adviser (Basic, Income), Commercial Non-life Insurance Adviser (Basic, Non-life Insurance Commercial, Non-life Insurance Personal), Mortgage Credit Adviser (Basic, Mortgage Credit, Wealth) and Pensions Adviser (Basic, Pension Insurance, Wealth). See the AFM professional competence requirements (in Dutch) and the CDFD advising authorities; the package check calculates it for you.

What happens if I do not complete my PE in time?

Then your advising authority for that professional qualification lapses. You restore it by passing a special exam or by retaking the initial module exams. Until then you may not advise independently in that field. So do not plan your PE exam at the last moment: towards the end of a period the exam institutes are busy.

Does a PE training count?

For the statutory advising authority only the PE exam you have passed counts; a training without an exam does not keep your diploma valid. Training can help you stay permanently up to date and sometimes counts towards register titles.

Does the PA test earn PE points or a PE exam?

No. The PA test is a practice test. You get a certificate with your result, but that is not evidence of professional competence and does not count towards your PE.

How do I practise for my PE exam for free?

On PA-WFT.nl there are multiple-choice questions with explanations per Wft module. A test on one module has 7 questions; each extra module adds 5. You can also make a mix of all modules in your own package. Free for all advisers, without an account.
