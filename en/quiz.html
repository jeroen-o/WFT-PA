Wft knowledge quiz

Seven questions for one module, five extra for every module you add, different every time, across the 9 Wft modules. For each answer you read why it is right or wrong, and afterwards you receive a certificate.

How the Wft knowledge quiz works

  1. Choose one or more Wft modules, or your whole package per professional qualification.
  2. Answer the multiple-choice questions: many are real-life cases, aligned with the exam terms of the CDFD from 1 April 2026.
  3. After each answer you read why each of the four answers is right or wrong.
  4. Afterwards you receive a certificate as a pdf, and you can practise your wrong questions again.

Frequently asked questions about the quiz

How many questions do I get?

With one module you get 7 multiple-choice questions. For each module you add, 5 more come in: 2 modules give 12 questions, 3 modules 17. The questions are chosen anew each time.

Is the test free?

Yes. The test and all modules are free for all advisers, without an account and without a subscription.

Does the test count towards my PE?

No. It is a practice test. It earns no PE points or PE exam. You keep your advising authority valid with a PE exam per professional qualification at a recognised exam institute.

What is on the certificate?

Your name, the modules, your score and the date, stating that it is a self-declaration for a practice test. Keep it in your personnel file and hand it to your employer.

Can I practise a specific topic?

Yes. On each module page you choose an exam term of the CDFD and practise only the questions about it. After the test you can also practise your wrong questions again.

How often should I take the test?

Take the test every quarter, so you stay permanently up to date. Set a reminder in your calendar with one click.
