Privacy

Privacy statement

Wft Permanent Actueel uses no cookies and no trackers. We only use your details to create your result and certificate; a copy of the certificate goes to DFO BV.

Who is responsible

DFO BV, De Wel 14–16, 3871 MV Hoevelaken, the Netherlands. Privacy questions: info@dfobv.nl.

The quiz and the certificate

To see your result you enter your name and email address and, if you wish, your date of birth, the company you work for and your employer’s email address for an extra copy. In doing so you agree to a copy of your certificate going to DFO BV. Your browser uses that data to create the certificate; PA-WFT.nl does not store it itself and does not send it to a server automatically. The certificate is prepared as a pdf in an email to yourself, with a copy to DFO BV (info@dfobv.nl). You send that message yourself from your email programme: if you do not send it, DFO BV receives nothing and you do not get the certificate in your mailbox. Practice rounds, such as practising your wrong questions again or practising per exam term, do not ask for any details. If you close the tab, the data is gone from your browser.

What DFO BV does with the copy

DFO BV receives the copy in the mailbox info@dfobv.nl. That is the data on the certificate: your name, email address, the result, the modules and the date, plus your date of birth, company and your employer’s email address if you entered them. The legal basis is your consent: you tick the box to agree to the copy. DFO BV does not keep the email longer than necessary and does not use the data for purposes other than confirming your participation in the practice test.

Cookies and measuring

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising services, or trackers. The font is hosted on our own site, so no data goes to external parties.

Hosting

This site does not place cookies, not even to remember choices. The site is hosted via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the pages. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask DFO BV to let you view, correct or delete the copy it has received from you: email info@dfobv.nl. If you have a question or complaint, email the same address. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
