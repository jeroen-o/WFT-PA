Wft Basic at a glance

  • Who needs it: required for 7 professional qualifications: Mortgage Credit Adviser, Wealth Adviser, Pensions Adviser, Consumer Credit Adviser, Commercial Non-life Insurance Adviser, Personal Non-life Insurance Adviser, Income Adviser.
  • PE: you take one PE exam per professional qualification within the PE period, which runs until 31 March 2028 inclusive.
  • Exam terms: the final objectives and exam terms of the CDFD have been renewed for exams from 1 April 2026.
  • Current: EU anti-money laundering package (AMLR and AMLD6): it comes on top of the current Wwft, which will in time be replaced by an implementation act.
  • Practise: 7 multiple-choice questions with an explanation for every answer, free and without an account. Last updated 18 October 2026.

What’s new in 2026

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • EU anti-money laundering package (AMLR and AMLD6). It comes on top of the current Wwft, which will in time be replaced by an implementation act. The gatekeeper role of advisers changes accordingly.
  • EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR) from 10 July 2027. The core customer due diligence rules will then sit in a directly applicable EU regulation. The new European anti-money laundering authority AMLA has been established and will directly supervise a limited number of large institutions; for advisory firms the national supervisor remains the point of contact.
  • DORA has applied since 17 January 2025. The European rules on digital operational resilience apply to, among others, banks and insurers. Insurance intermediaries that are micro, small or medium-sized enterprises fall outside it, but they notice it through their suppliers and principals. The Wft requirement of controlled business operations still applies to ICT risks.
  • AI Act. Phased application affects advisory firms through software, models and outsourcing.

Example questions Wft Basic

This is what the quiz questions look like. In the quiz the answers appear in random order.

Example question 1 exam term 1f.8

Sandra Mulder owns an advisory firm that advises on around two hundred mortgages a year. A lender phones her with an offer. If the firm places at least fifty mortgages with him this year, two advisers may go on a study trip to Lisbon free of charge. Sandra wonders whether she can accept. What is correct?

  • This is not permitted, because a benefit in kind from the provider also falls under the commission ban
  • This is permitted if the firm clearly states the trip in the services document
  • This is permitted, as long as the firm's clients pay nothing extra for it
  • This is permitted, because it concerns a trip and not a sum of money
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: This is not permitted, because a benefit in kind from the provider also falls under the commission ban. For mortgage credit an adviser may not receive commission from the provider, not even in kind. A trip that depends on the number of mortgages is moreover a strong incentive against the client's interest.

  • This is permitted, because it concerns a trip and not a sum of money: The commission ban also applies to benefits in kind.
  • This is permitted, as long as the firm's clients pay nothing extra for it: What matters is the incentive for the adviser, not what the client pays.
  • This is permitted if the firm clearly states the trip in the services document: Transparency does not make a prohibited benefit permitted.

Example question 2 exam term 1d.13

Dennis Kuipers (34) started his own business as a carpenter three years ago. He works as a self-employed person without staff for various clients and has no employees. After a fall from scaffolding he realises he does not know what happens if he cannot work for a long time. He asks which statutory provision protects him. What is correct?

  • As a self-employed person he does not in principle fall under the WIA and must cover this risk himself
  • He automatically falls under the WIA as soon as he is registered with the Chamber of Commerce
  • He is entitled to a WW (unemployment benefit) for as long as he cannot work
  • He receives an AOW (state pension) benefit as soon as he becomes incapacitated for work
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: As a self-employed person he does not in principle fall under the WIA and must cover this risk himself. The WIA is an employee insurance. A self-employed person can cover the risk, for example, with disability insurance or a mutual self-employed support fund (broodfonds).

  • He automatically falls under the WIA as soon as he is registered with the Chamber of Commerce: Registration with the Chamber of Commerce does not make him an employee; the WIA applies to employees.
  • He receives an AOW (state pension) benefit as soon as he becomes incapacitated for work: The AOW is an old-age provision from the AOW age.
  • He is entitled to a WW (unemployment benefit) for as long as he cannot work: The WW is an employee insurance for unemployment, not for incapacity for work.

Example question 3 exam term 1a.7

Willem Dijkstra (47) is married to Saskia. His best friend Paul wants to take out a €15,000.00 loan to buy a camper van. The bank wants Willem to stand personal surety for that loan. Willem wants to do so without involving Saskia. What applies in principle?

  • Consent is not needed, because each spouse may perform legal acts independently
  • Consent is only needed if Willem and Saskia are married in community of property
  • He needs Saskia's consent; without that consent she can annul the suretyship
  • Only Paul's bank has to approve the suretyship in advance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: He needs Saskia's consent; without that consent she can annul the suretyship. A personal suretyship requires the consent of the spouse. If it is missing, Saskia can annul the suretyship.

  • Consent is not needed, because each spouse may perform legal acts independently: For suretyship the law in fact makes an exception: the spouse's consent is needed for it.
  • Consent is only needed if Willem and Saskia are married in community of property: The consent requirement applies regardless of the matrimonial property regime.
  • Only Paul's bank has to approve the suretyship in advance: The bank assesses the surety, but that does not replace the spouse's consent.
