Wft Income at a glance

  • Who needs it: required for 1 professional qualification: Income Adviser.
  • PE: you take one PE exam per professional qualification within the PE period, which runs until 31 March 2028 inclusive.
  • Exam terms: the final objectives and exam terms of the CDFD have been renewed for exams from 1 April 2026. One exam term for this module was changed.
  • Current: Disability insurance for the self-employed: a compulsory basic insurance is in preparation.
  • Practise: 7 multiple-choice questions with an explanation for every answer, free and without an account. Last updated 18 October 2026.

What is covered

  • Continued payment of wages during sickness and the Gatekeeper Improvement Act

What’s new in 2026

  • New CDFD exam terms from 1 April 2026. The final objectives and exam terms for this module have been renewed and apply to exams from 1 April 2026. The quiz questions are aligned with them.
  • Changed exam term. Exam term 1h.8 moved from knowledge level to understanding level on 1 April 2026: you must not only be able to describe the legal frameworks for asking for medical information, medical examination limits and medical acceptance, but also explain and apply them in a concrete situation, such as hereditary conditions and a health declaration or medical examination.
  • Disability insurance for the self-employed. A compulsory basic insurance is in preparation. Follow the status of the bill; the current rules remain in force until introduction.
  • Enforcement against bogus self-employment since 1 January 2025. The Tax Administration enforces the assessment of employment relationships again. If a freelancer is in reality an employee, the employee insurances apply. Discuss with freelance clients how certain their status is; it affects their income risk and the choice of disability insurance.

Example questions Wft Income

This is what the quiz questions look like. In the quiz the answers appear in random order.

Example question 1 exam term 1i.2

Hans is a technical draughtsman and reaches the AOW age this year. He earned €3,800 gross per month and will receive a modest pension on top. His wife Ellen is eight years younger and has no income of her own. Their children already live independently. What is the starting point for their AOW?

  • Ellen also receives AOW immediately because her husband receives AOW
  • Hans receives a partner supplement from the SVB until Ellen receives AOW
  • Hans receives an AOW for married people; there is no longer a partner supplement for Ellen
  • Hans receives an AOW for single people until Ellen reaches the AOW age
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: Hans receives an AOW for married people; there is no longer a partner supplement for Ellen. The AOW partner supplement was abolished as of 2015; the household may therefore have an income gap until Ellen herself receives AOW.

  • Hans receives an AOW for single people until Ellen reaches the AOW age: Married people receive the AOW for married people, even if the partner does not yet receive AOW.
  • Ellen also receives AOW immediately because her husband receives AOW: AOW is a personal right that starts at one's own AOW age.
  • Hans receives a partner supplement from the SVB until Ellen receives AOW: The partner supplement no longer exists for anyone who became entitled to AOW after 2014.

Example question 2 exam term 1i.8

Erik (37) works as a cook in a restaurant and earns €2,700 gross per month. He is married and has a four-year-old daughter. He is sick for four weeks with pneumonia, then works fully for three weeks and then drops out with back problems. His employer wonders whether the period for continued payment of wages starts again. How do these periods count for the continued payment of wages?

  • A new period of 104 weeks starts, because the complaints are different
  • The first period lapses, because Erik was reported fully recovered
  • They are added together, because the interruption is shorter than four weeks
  • They are only added together if the company doctor establishes the same cause
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: They are added together, because the interruption is shorter than four weeks. Periods of sickness that follow each other with an interruption of less than four weeks are added together, regardless of the cause.

  • A new period of 104 weeks starts, because the complaints are different: The cause is irrelevant for adding together; only the length of the interruption counts.
  • They are only added together if the company doctor establishes the same cause: Adding together does not depend on the same cause, but on an interruption shorter than four weeks.
  • The first period lapses, because Erik was reported fully recovered: A recovery report only breaks the chain if the employee works again for at least four weeks.

Example question 3 exam term 1g.3

Karin (39) is a self-employed physiotherapist and makes €58,000 profit per year. She is married and has a six-year-old daughter. Her AOV has a threshold of 25% and pays out pro rata to the degree of incapacity for work. After a serious herniated neck disc the insurer determines that she is 60% incapacitated for work. What does she receive in principle?

  • A benefit of 25% of the insured amount
  • A benefit that corresponds to 60% incapacity for work, not the full amount
  • Nothing, because she is not fully incapacitated for work
  • The full insured amount, because she is well above the policy threshold of 25%
Show the answer and explanation

Correct: A benefit that corresponds to 60% incapacity for work, not the full amount. With pro rata payment the insured receives a part of the insured amount that corresponds to the degree of incapacity for work.

  • The full insured amount, because she is well above the policy threshold of 25%: Paying out above the threshold does not automatically mean a full benefit.
  • Nothing, because she is not fully incapacitated for work: The 25% threshold has been reached, so she is entitled to a benefit.
  • A benefit of 25% of the insured amount: The threshold determines when a benefit is paid, not how much.
